## Evidence classification

- an originating source in Civilization A,
- a transmitted signal/influence,
- receipt by Civilization B.

This statement does NOT, by itself, establish that the human witness recognized:

- that Civilization B's local change was caused by that received influence,
- that the civilizations never physically touched,
- that Civilization B authored its own local response rather than being directly mutated by Civilization A,
- the exact Living World → NaShaTa → Underflow → NaShaTa → receiving-world route.

Those unspoken elements remain unclaimed.

2. local authorship in Civilization B,
